# MedTech local technology photography

The MedTech homepage uses `lib/medtech-showcase.json` for its hero spotlight and
photo gallery. LifeTech does not render these sections. Original downloaded
images are stored in `assets/images/medtech-showcase/`; each record retains the
publisher's page, image URL, credit, dimensions, and date checked (2026-10-03).
Images are presented intact, with CSS containing them within the card frame.
Credits link to the original publication. These are publisher photographs and
one explicitly labeled conceptual illustration, not generated product photos.

Selection began with the checked-in local organization index at
`assets/data/ecosystem.json`:

- Harbor Designs & Manufacturing: Hemafuse manufacturing components, from its
  own medical-device portfolio. Selected for a clear close-up of actual parts.
- RPM Tech: Qvella FAST ID instrument, from its own diagnostics engineering
  portfolio. Selected for an unobstructed product view. Its published image is
  400 × 267; it is retained at its original resolution.
- Salynt: its own four-view brain illustration. The site identifies this as a
  conceptual illustration, not a patient scan or product screenshot. SmartMed
  is described as research-stage.
- Galen Robotics was discovered through the index's Johns Hopkins Technology
  Ventures / FastForward entry. JHTV's article documents the Baltimore location and Hopkins
  technology connection; Xometry's official robotics page publishes the robot
  photograph. The picture is a demonstration on a mannequin, not a surgical
  procedure or a claim about the current Galen ES model. Galen is not claimed
  to be an existing standalone entry in the source workbook.

The separate **Inside local imaging suites** group provides the requested
CT/CAT and MRI hardware photographs at Baltimore's University of Maryland
Medical Center. The CT photo comes from Turner's UMMC renovation portfolio;
the MRI photo comes from SETTY's UMMC engineering portfolio. Both show clinical
settings, not locally manufactured scanners. UMMC is not equated with the
index's University of Maryland, Baltimore / UM Ventures / FastForward entry. Turner and SETTY
are credited publishers, not new organizations added to the workbook.

Source attribution records provenance; it does not assert an open image license,
company endorsement, or a partnership with Baltimore MedTech. The original
source URLs and credits stay with the images when the gallery is changed.
