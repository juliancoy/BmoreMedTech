A shared regional language

Three terms.
One local context.

These definitions are specific to the Baltimore regional medical community. They explain how Baltimore MedTech distinguishes three closely connected fields in its work.

01

Systems & institutions

Health

Health, as in public health or healthcare, refers to the organizational and financial concerns of big medicine: insurance, hospitals, and outreach. It also includes Epic, CMS, and other business functions.

02

Immediate patient care

Medicine

Medicine is separated into diagnostic and operative practice. It includes the immediate, individual care given to a patient.

03

Life science & industry

Biotech

Biotech largely covers organic drugs and biopharmaceuticals, but can also include genetics. It frequently employs the capitalist mode of production.
